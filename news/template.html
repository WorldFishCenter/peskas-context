      About Peskas

      Peskas is WorldFish's open-source platform for small-scale fisheries data, run with national fisheries agencies and research partners. Enumerators record what boats land; Peskas checks those records, estimates catch, value and fishing activity for the whole fleet, and shares the results through public dashboards, a regional portal and a data API.

      This bulletin covers Peskas in Timor-Leste, Kenya, Zanzibar and Mozambique and the Peskas Coasts regional portal. It is written each month from the release notes the Peskas team publishes with every software update.
